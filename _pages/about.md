At McGill and Mila, I am supervised by [Prof. Blake Richards](https://mila.quebec/en/directory/blake-richards). I am also interested in using virtual and augmented reality alongside EEG and fNIRS to collect richer data for neuroscience.

Before my PhD, I completed an MSc in Neuro-X at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), supervised by [Prof. Alexander Mathis](https://mathislab.org/). For my [master’s thesis]({% post_url 2026-11-25-lever-press-neural-timescales %}), I studied temporal abstraction in foraging macaques, asking which timescales of behavior best predict prefrontal activity. I earned my BSc in Neuroscience with a minor in Computer Science at McGill under the supervision of Prof. Blake Richards and [Prof. Jonathan Cornford](https://eps.leeds.ac.uk/computing/staff/15737/dr-jonathan-cornford).
