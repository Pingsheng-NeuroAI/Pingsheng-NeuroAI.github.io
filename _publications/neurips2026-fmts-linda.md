Abstract: LINDA learns shared dynamics from neural recordings that capture different parts of a system at different times. It uses the recent history of neural activity to predict how the latent state evolves, allowing the dynamics to change within a trial. On synthetic data, LINDA recovered common dynamics from different observations of the same system. On recordings from monkeys performing a reaching task, combining self-supervised prediction with supervised decoding improved transfer to a new animal when little training data was available.
