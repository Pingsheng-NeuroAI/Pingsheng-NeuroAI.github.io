LINDA

I developed LINDA to learn patterns shared across neural recordings. It uses a joint embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) to predict how neural activity will evolve. With just two labeled trials from a new monkey, adding this prediction task improved how accurately the model decoded reach velocity.

Object recognition in VR

At SilicoLabs, I built a Unity experiment and its data collection and analysis pipeline. Participants could turn unfamiliar objects before classifying them. I proposed replaying their chosen views to a vision model to compare how people and models learn.
