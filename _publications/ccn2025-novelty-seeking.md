Abstract: Can an agent learn to represent independent factors in its environment by choosing what to explore? In a multi-armed bandit task, an agent used the novelty of observations in an autoencoder’s latent space as a reward. This led it to sample data in which the underlying factors varied independently and helped it learn representations that separated those factors.
